# RMT2 proof-pair retention contract

Version `hxfail5-pcnt2m1-rmt2` changes evidence retention, not HX sampling or acceptance. The three RMT roles are now FIRST_LINK, FIRST_BAD_PROOF and FIRST_GOOD_PROOF. RMT schema2 / outer schema6 / `DUAL600_FRAME_PCNT_RMT_PROOF_PAIR` keep the same64 metadata words,190 words per trace,634-word block, three slots, two working buffers and≤4096-byte setup-only allocation.

BAD/GOOD come from the original `hxpAddProof` snapshot's strict valid0/1, with the newest actual proof sequence equal to the completed RAW sequence. The complete packed units, bits, accepted and postcheck must match. Global `proof_ready` and `hxapUsable` are deliberately not selection prerequisites: proof-source frames normally carry NO_PROOF, and one bad proof keeps globalready0 even when a later individual proof is valid. Neither original validity predicate nor bad-proof stickiness changed. GOOD is a strict original counter proof, never ADC truth or calibration approval.

FIRST_BAD/GOOD lock even if no RX job exists, or it times out, is partial, has the wrong callback buffer, or has uncertain shape. They retain the first sequence with NA/reason; they do not select a later healthy trace. LINK and BAD may be the same frame; identical canonical traces are checked and counted once. GPIO stage4 now belongs to first BAD. GOOD keeps the existing READY/pre-arm safety scope; no new post-event GPIO snapshot is invented.

The PROOF_SOURCE policy bit now marks an actual active proof transaction, including a real failed proof transaction. It is no longer set merely because execution entered the proof-preparation branch after the eight-proof limit. This corrects diagnostic bookkeeping only. NO_PROOF, RAW, clocks, calibration guards and the original maximum-eight/strict-ready behavior remain unchanged. Historical RMT1 bit semantics and files remain readable.

The parser checks roles against the full405-word PCNT proof table, including index, seq, valid, bits, accepted/postcheck and every packed unit word. Early proof frames may have rolled out of both RAW rings; that does not waive a missing RMT role. Embedded RAW/PCNT are also checked against every retained intersection. New output cannot masquerade as schema5/old RMT roles.

There is one explicit retention-boundary exception. The original RAW observer decides retention by read_start, not read_end. If setup crosses a deadline, an actual proof can be appended while the RAW frame itself is omitted. No RAW/event is fabricated. A missing role is reported as `RAW_UNRETAINED_AT_DEADLINE` only with all of: configured RMT; NOT_BOUND terminal; actual first-role proof is the final proof; seq equals RMT attempts/lastseq and frozen LINK attempts; exactly one omitted attempt; seq absent from all retained RAW; empty role metadata/payload; genuine WINDOW≥600s or POST≥20s with a real trigger and WINDOW priority; compatible available API CPU bounds. Otherwise missing first BAD/GOOD is rejected. The exception reports unavailable waveform evidence and does not mean untriggered or passed.

Host evidence includes normal7GOOD/1BAD, allGOOD, allBAD, BAD→GOOD with globalready0, LINK=BAD canonical deduplication, natural600s noLINK export, sidecar/RMT allocation failures, missing-RX/timeout/old-buffer first-role immutability and original-PCNT/RMT/Observe deadline cases. Synthetic flags and current exporter hashes are explicit. No physical waveform or measurement acceptance follows from host results.

Final device QC must verify the exact build/write/boot identity, GPIO, WiFi and post-allocation heap; original strict proof outcomes; first BAD/GOOD selected sequences and finite symbols/NA; all CRCs and overall END; and added timing overhead. Preserve endpoint, cross-channel offset and hidden-RX_ERROR limitations. RMT1 evidence remains historical. No TARE/CAL366 is permitted merely because this retention change compiles or later frames look normal.
